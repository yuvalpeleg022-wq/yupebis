# Director Sub-Skill: Sanjay Leela Bhansali
*Load with the base `filmcraft` SKILL.md.*
*Devdas · Bajirao Mastani · Padmaavat · Black · Ram-Leela · Gangubai Kathiawadi*

**Core obsession / worldview.** Doomed love rendered as opera. His subject is grand, thwarted passion — lovers separated by class, faith, honour, or death — staged with zero interest in realism and total confidence in artifice. His frames are "frozen in amber," built with the attention to detail of paintings. Emotion is always at its operatic extreme; his characters are usually destroyed by the very intensity that makes them worth watching. The tragedy is the point.

**Working method.** Total authorship, including the music. He **composes his own soundtracks** — produces, directs, writes, edits, *and* scores (e.g. *Gangubai Kathiawadi*) — so melody and mise-en-scène are conceived together. A notorious perfectionist who builds enormous physical sets rather than shooting locations, controls colour palettes obsessively, and directs emotion through **close-ups, slow-motion, and light** so the audience "feels every tear, smile, and glance." He and his DP engineer a specific "Bhansali look" — often daylight manufactured indoors, jewel-toned and painterly.

**Signature (spottable).** Operatic maximalism (palatial sets, mirrored halls, hundreds of diyas/candles, mounted spectacle); saturated single-colour flooding (Ram-Leela's reds, Bajirao's gold mirror-hall in "Deewani Mastani"); self-composed classically-rooted songs staged as full set-pieces that carry the tragedy; slow-motion + close-up on the wound; symmetry and deep-focus painterly depth (doorways within doorways).

**Defining films.** *Devdas* (2002) — tragic romance raised to operatic grandeur; his maximalism fully arrives · *Black* (2005) — his most restrained and critically decorated; the emotion works without the palace · *Bajirao Mastani* (2015) — the peak of his set-piece craft · *Padmaavat* (2018) — his biggest-scale doomed-honour epic (and biggest controversy) · *Goliyon Ki Raasleela Ram-Leela* (2013) — *Romeo & Juliet* in saturated colour; launched the Deepika–Ranveer pairing · *Gangubai Kathiawadi* (2022) — full authorship (he scored it too); a late-career high.

**Collaborators.** DP Sudeep Chatterjee (*Bajirao Mastani, Padmaavat, Gangubai*) — architect of the "Bhansali look"; earlier Binod Pradhan (*Devdas*), Ravi Varman (*Ram-Leela*). Stars Deepika Padukone and Ranveer Singh (his repertory couple), Aishwarya Rai, Alia Bhatt. Composes his own music (with lyricists like A.M. Turaz).

**The one lesson to steal.** Commit totally to artifice — decide the emotional temperature first, then bend colour, set, light, and a song you conceived yourself to serve it. Realism is optional; conviction is not.

**Weakness / criticism.** Style over substance; thin characters under the opulence. The relentless grandeur can swallow the story — gorgeous but airless, spectacle substituting for psychological depth. Women are frequently sacrificial icons of honour rather than agents (the *jauhar* climax of *Padmaavat* drew heavy criticism). His historical films provoke accusations of distortion and reactionary gender politics dressed as tragedy.
