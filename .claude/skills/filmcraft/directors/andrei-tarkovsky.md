# Director Sub-Skill: Andrei Tarkovsky
*Load with the base `filmcraft` SKILL.md. Channel this to write, shoot, or critique in Tarkovsky's mode — or to answer "how would Tarkovsky do X."*

**Core obsession / worldview.** Faith, memory, sacrifice, and the spiritual crisis of materialist modernity; the artist's duty; nature and water as the medium of memory and the sacred. Time itself as cinema's true subject — "sculpting in time."

**Working method.** Theorized the shot's *duration and internal rhythm* as everything; refused conventional montage-driven editing. Extremely long takes with slow, motivated camera movement. Meticulous control of the elemental (water, fire, mud, wind). Cast for spiritual presence over technique. Fought Soviet censorship constantly (*Andrei Rublev* suppressed for years). Reshot heavily — *Stalker* was made almost twice after the first footage was ruined (the toxic location may have caused later cancers among the crew).

**Signature (spottable).** The very long, slow take (some run minutes). Camera drifting laterally or forward through detailed environments. The recurring image-kit: water (running, flooding rooms, submerged objects), fire, indoor rain, wind through fields, milk, mirrors, levitation, dogs, dripping. Desaturation slipping between B&W, sepia, and color to mark memory/dream/reality. Ambient, unearthly sound (Artemyev's electronics). Zone/threshold spaces. No exposition — associative dream logic.

**Defining films.** *Andrei Rublev* (1966) · *Solaris* (1972) · *Mirror* (1975) · *Stalker* (1979) · *Nostalghia* (1983) · *The Sacrifice* (1986); debut *Ivan's Childhood* (1962).

**Key collaborators.** DPs Vadim Yusov (early), Georgy Rerberg (*Mirror*), Aleksandr Knyazhinsky (*Stalker*); composer Eduard Artemyev; writers Arkady & Boris Strugatsky (*Stalker*); actor Anatoly Solonitsyn; in exile, DP Sven Nykvist (*The Sacrifice*) and co-writer Tonino Guerra.

**The one lesson to steal.** Duration is expressive — let a shot breathe far past the "efficient" cut and time becomes felt, spiritual, real. Sound and nature are metaphysics, not ambience.

**Weakness / honest criticism.** Slow to the point of ordeal for most audiences; solemn, mystical, humorless. The spiritual grandiosity and self-image of the suffering visionary (his diaries veer into martyrdom) can feel self-regarding. Narrative is often opaque to inaccessibility; the women are thin (idealized/suffering).

Sources: Tarkovsky, *Sculpting in Time* (essential) and his diaries *Time Within Time* · *Voyage in Time* (with Antonioni) · Michal Leszczylowski's *Directed by Andrei Tarkovsky* · Robert Bird, *Andrei Tarkovsky: Elements of Cinema*.
