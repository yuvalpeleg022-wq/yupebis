# Director Sub-Skill: Wes Anderson
*Load with the base `filmcraft` SKILL.md.*

**Core obsession / worldview.** Deadpan melancholy inside a dollhouse — grief, broken families, and arrested-development adults dressed in precise, whimsical worlds. Beneath the symmetry and pastel is real sadness: dead parents, failed fathers, lonely children, wounded siblings. The control *is* the theme — obsessive order as a defense against chaos and loss.

**Working method.** Total authorial control. Extreme visual symmetry and flat, frontal **planimetric** staging — camera perpendicular to the set, depth minimized, the frame reads like an illustration; characters move on horizontal/vertical rails. Curated, harmonized color palettes and graphic production design. Performances choreographed to deadpan flatness that keeps hyper-stylized sets from tipping into camp. Storyboards and animatics for everything; a loyal repertory shot in a communal, hotel-living production style.

**Signature (spottable).** Dead-center symmetrical framing; snap-zooms and whip-pans; 90° overhead insert shots of objects/hands; slow-motion set to '60s–'70s pop needle-drops; chapter titles, narrators, and books/plays/miniatures as framing devices; whimsical cutaways; a dollhouse lateral track past cross-sectioned rooms; Futura/typewriter typography.

**Defining films.** *Rushmore* (1998) — the deadpan-melancholy voice arrives · *The Royal Tenenbaums* (2001) — family grief in a symmetrical dollhouse; the template · *The Life Aquatic* (2004) — the cross-section set as pure Anderson · *Fantastic Mr. Fox* (2009) — stop-motion proved his style is medium-agnostic · *Moonrise Kingdom* (2012) — young love at its most controlled and tender · *The Grand Budapest Hotel* (2014) — the aesthetic and emotional peak; nested narration, aspect-ratio shifts · *The French Dispatch* / *Asteroid City* (2021/2023) — the style at maximum density.

**Key collaborators.** Co-writers Roman Coppola, Jason Schwartzman, Hugo Guinness, Noah Baumbach. DP Robert Yeoman (every live-action film). Composer Alexandre Desplat (recent era). Editors Barney Pilling / Andrew Weisblum. Repertory cast: Bill Murray, Owen Wilson, Jason Schwartzman, Tilda Swinton, Willem Dafoe, Adrien Brody, Jeff Goldblum, Frances McDormand.

**The one lesson to steal.** A rigidly consistent visual system is itself an emotional statement. Total control of frame, color, and rhythm can *express* the character's inner order-against-chaos — style as content, not decoration.

**Weakness / honest criticism.** "Style over substance" is the standing charge — the constructed, synthetic frame can hold viewers at a cold, detached distance rather than moving them. Emotional flatness by design reads to critics as preciousness or twee. Later films (*French Dispatch*, *Asteroid City*) drew complaints of self-parody — the dollhouse so dense it suffocates the feeling it's meant to protect. Limited demographic/tonal range.
