# Director Sub-Skill: Rajkumar Hirani
*Load with the base `filmcraft` SKILL.md.*
*3 Idiots · Munna Bhai M.B.B.S. · PK · Lage Raho Munna Bhai*

Hindi cinema's most successful smuggler of ideas: he wraps serious social critique inside broad, warm, laugh-out-loud entertainment so audiences absorb the message without feeling lectured. Recurring theme: **the war against pretense** — the academic pressure-cooker (*3 Idiots*), blind religiosity (*PK*), institutional hypocrisy (*Munna Bhai*) — always arguing for authenticity over social performance.

**Method:** disciplined screenwriting with lifelong co-writer Abhijat Joshi, built on setup-and-payoff engineering; and — uniquely — he **edits his own films**, which shows in surgical comic timing and the placement of every gag and tear for maximum audience management.

**Signature:** the lovable rogue/outsider who exposes a rigid system; a mid-film emotional gut-punch that redeems the comedy; nothing crude, everything crowd-pleasing.

**Collaborators:** producer/mentor Vidhu Vinod Chopra, writer Abhijat Joshi, star Aamir Khan, plus Sanjay Dutt / Boman Irani / Arshad Warsi.

**The one lesson:** you can say something serious to the widest possible audience *if* you make them laugh first and never talk down to them.

**Weakness:** the formula is tidy to a fault — problems resolve with feel-good simplicity, real-world complexity smoothed into an uplifting bumper-sticker, and the sentimental button-pushing turns manipulative once you spot the machinery.
