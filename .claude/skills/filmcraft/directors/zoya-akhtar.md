# Director Sub-Skill: Zoya Akhtar
*Load with the base `filmcraft` SKILL.md.*
*Gully Boy · Zindagi Na Milegi Dobara · Dil Dhadakne Do · The Archies*

The sharpest chronicler of modern Indian aspiration and class, working almost always through **urban ensembles** whose multiple character arcs she braids together (*ZNMD*'s three friends, *Dil Dhadakne Do*'s dysfunctional rich family, *Gully Boy*'s Mumbai rappers). Her breakthrough gave privileged self-discovery a glossy, emotionally literate frame; her peak (*Gully Boy*) turned outward to the underclass, dramatizing a slum kid's rise through the real Mumbai gully-rap scene as both music film and class critique.

**Method:** writes with longtime collaborator Reema Kagti; favors naturalistic performance and lived-in production detail; lets soundtrack define a subculture.

**Collaborators:** co-writer Reema Kagti, brother/actor-writer Farhan Akhtar and father-lyricist Javed Akhtar, producer Ritesh Sidhwani (Excel Entertainment), DPs Carlos Catalán and Jay Oza.

**The one lesson:** an ensemble lets you make a film about a whole world, not one hero — give each character a real want and the theme emerges from the friction between them.

**Weakness:** her gaze started (and often stays) inside privilege; even her outward-looking films are directed *at* the underclass from a comfortable distance, and the ensemble structure can dilute into pleasant, well-appointed drift when no single arc is urgent enough.
