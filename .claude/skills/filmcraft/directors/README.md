# Director Sub-Skills — Index

Each file is a self-contained working profile of one filmmaker: core obsession, working method, spottable signature, defining films, key collaborators, the one lesson to steal, and an honest weakness. Load **one** file when the user names that director, wants to write/shoot/critique in their mode, or asks "how would [X] do this." Load with the base `filmcraft` SKILL.md for the craft vocabulary underneath.

The one-paragraph figures still without their own file (Ozu, Coppola, Lynch, Gerwig, Peele, del Toro, Zhao, plus craft minds Deakins, Murch, Zimmer, Morricone) live in `../references/director-psychology.md`. Promote any of them to a full file on the same template when needed.

## Global (contemporary)
- [Christopher Nolan](christopher-nolan.md) — time/memory puzzle-box, IMAX & practical, structure-is-content.
- [Martin Scorsese](martin-scorsese.md) — Catholic guilt, kinetic camera, needle-drops, let the audience enjoy the sin.
- [Quentin Tarantino](quentin-tarantino.md) — cinema-about-cinema, dialogue as suspense, chaptered revenge.
- [Denis Villeneuve](denis-villeneuve.md) — awe and dread, image over word, scale creates emotion.
- [Bong Joon-ho](bong-joon-ho.md) — class/systems, tonal whiplash, theme in the architecture.
- [Steven Spielberg](steven-spielberg.md) — wonder/childhood, blocking mastery, direct the emotion not the info.
- [Hayao Miyazaki](hayao-miyazaki.md) — flight/nature/pacifism, hand-drawn, "ma" (stillness).
- [Wes Anderson](wes-anderson.md) — dollhouse melancholy, planimetric symmetry, style as content.
- [David Fincher](david-fincher.md) — obsession/process, digital precision, repetition strips performance to truth.
- [Paul Thomas Anderson](paul-thomas-anderson.md) — broken fathers/ambition, move-then-hold camera, build around a performance.
- [The Coen Brothers](coen-brothers.md) — fate & cosmic indifference, storyboard-exact, comedy and dread in one frame.
- [Park Chan-wook](park-chan-wook.md) — revenge & beauty/brutality, designed set-pieces, structure as revelation.
- [Alfonso Cuarón](alfonso-cuaron.md) — rebirth through crisis, the immersive long take, technique in service of empathy.
- [Pedro Almodóvar](pedro-almodovar.md) — desire & melodrama, saturated red, take heightened emotion seriously.

## Classical & world masters
- [Alfred Hitchcock](alfred-hitchcock.md) — guilt & voyeurism, suspense = audience knows the bomb, solve it on paper.
- [Stanley Kubrick](stanley-kubrick.md) — reason vs. the void, symmetrical one-point frame, research yields unseen images.
- [Akira Kurosawa](akira-kurosawa.md) — moral duty in violent systems, multi-camera long lens, weather is dramaturgy.
- [Ingmar Bergman](ingmar-bergman.md) — God's silence & the face, chamber drama, hold on the face past comfort.
- [Federico Fellini](federico-fellini.md) — memory/dream/carnival, studio artifice, feeling outranks realism.
- [Andrei Tarkovsky](andrei-tarkovsky.md) — faith & memory, "sculpting in time," duration is expressive.
- [Wong Kar-wai](wong-kar-wai.md) — missed connection/time, smeared slow-mo & obstructed frames, mood carries narrative.
- [Abbas Kiarostami](abbas-kiarostami.md) — fiction/documentary blur, cars & long roads, the unshown is the most powerful.
- [Agnès Varda](agnes-varda.md) — the overlooked, "cinécriture," the small camera and the maker's presence are the honesty.

## Indian
- [Imtiaz Ali](imtiaz-ali.md) — journey/self-discovery romance, music as narrative, the restless protagonist.
- [S.S. Rajamouli](ss-rajamouli.md) — pan-Indian mythic epic, the elevation moment, emotion at max amplitude.
- [Sanjay Leela Bhansali](sanjay-leela-bhansali.md) — operatic doomed love, self-composed music, total artifice.
- [Anurag Kashyap](anurag-kashyap.md) — Indian neo-noir, real-location grit, cast for truth not wattage.
- [Satyajit Ray](satyajit-ray.md) — humanist master, restraint, the small gesture carries the emotion.
- [Rajkumar Hirani](rajkumar-hirani.md) — social message inside mainstream comedy, make them laugh first.
- [Mani Ratnam](mani-ratnam.md) — political romance, rim-light beauty, discovered A.R. Rahman.
- [Zoya Akhtar](zoya-akhtar.md) — urban ensemble realism, a film about a world not one hero.
- [Guru Dutt](guru-dutt.md) — the tragic poet, expressionist light, the song as the soul made visible.
