# Director Sub-Skill: Bong Joon-ho
*Load with the base `filmcraft` SKILL.md.*

**Core obsession / worldview.** Class and systems — the machinery of inequality and the people crushed inside it (the train's cars in *Snowpiercer*, the floors of a house in *Parasite*, the food chain in *Okja*). Refuses moral simplicity: villains are sympathetic, victims complicit, no one clean. Genre is a Trojan horse for social critique.

**Working method.** Obsessive storyboarder — nicknamed "Bong-tae" (Bong + storyboard); boards films panel by panel, working out shots and spatial logic before rolling. Almost nothing improvised on set; the film is largely "cut" on paper. Insists a film's genre shouldn't constrain it.

**Signature (spottable).** The vertical axis as pure metaphor — who is above and who is below is never accidental (*Parasite*'s semi-basement vs. hilltop mansion vs. bunker). Tonal whiplash — comedy sliding into horror into tragedy within one scene, achieved through "situation, visuals, lighting, and camera movement," not performance. Precise geometric blocking; sudden bursts of slapstick and violence; deep-focus staging that hides who's really in control.

**Defining films.** *Memories of Murder* (2003) — where he began storyboarding; an unsolved-murder procedural about institutional failure · *The Host* (2006) — a monster movie really about family and government incompetence · *Mother* (2009) — a mother's love pushed past morality · *Snowpiercer* (2013) — class war as a literal train; his English-language leap · *Okja* (2017) — capitalism and animal ethics as adventure · *Parasite* (2019) — the tonal-tightrope masterpiece; first non-English Best Picture winner.

**Key collaborators.** DP Hong Kyung-pyo (*Snowpiercer*, *Parasite*, *Mother*). Editor Yang Jin-mo (*Okja*, *Parasite*). Composer Jung Jae-il (*Okja*, *Parasite*). Recurring actor Song Kang-ho (four films). Writing partner Han Jin-won on *Parasite*.

**The one lesson to steal.** Put your theme in the architecture. Let physical space — up/down, inside/outside, forward/back — carry the meaning so you never have to state it.

**Weakness / honest criticism.** The tonal shifts that thrill some viewers jar others — comedy can undercut horror or vice versa. Social messaging occasionally turns didactic (the eco/anti-capitalist beats in *Okja*, *Snowpiercer*). Because the film is so fully pre-planned, performances can feel subordinated to the diagram. *Mickey 17* drew "diminishing returns" grumbles.
