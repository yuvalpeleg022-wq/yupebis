# Real Player Database — Import Report

Season: **2026-27** (career start 2026-09-01). Research date: 2026-11-03. Generated by `pnpm report:coaches`.

## How this data was obtained

- The licensed open sources for player facts (Wikidata CC0, Wikipedia CC BY-SA) and official league sites (NBA.com, EuroLeague.net) were **blocked by this session's network policy**. A GitHub-hosted dataset scraped from Basketball-Reference was reachable but has no licence and conflicts with that site's terms, so it was **not used**.
- Rosters were researched through web-search summaries of official club announcements and sports media. Primary pages were not opened.
- **Inclusion rule:** a player is imported only when the club assignment is `official` (a club or league announcement is cited) or `cross_checked` (at least two independent sources agree). Single-source mentions are listed as candidates; contradicted assignments are excluded as conflicts.
- No source provided birth dates, heights, weights, nationalities or statistics, so these fields are **empty** for every imported player. Ratings are therefore **provisional** (league baseline adjusted only by a source-reported starter/rotation/reserve role) and marked in the UI.

## Roster completeness definition

- **Complete (simulation-ready):** at least the league roster minimum of real players (NBA 13, EuroLeague 11, FIBA domestic 10).
- **Short:** 8 or more real players but below the minimum.
- **Missing:** fewer than 8 real players.
- A competition is **ready to simulate in Real Players Only mode** only when every participating club is complete.
